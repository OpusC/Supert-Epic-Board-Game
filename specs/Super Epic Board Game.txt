Brief: cooperative RPG where you pick characters to fight against monsters


Mechanics
* Divided into 4 acts (1 starting, and 3 normal)
   * Roll a die to determine which encounter or event
   * Each act require a minimum number of encounters to face the boss
* 2 rows on the player side, with 3 slots each row
   * Players in the front will take the damage, shielding the player behind
   * If the player behind has nobody in front, they still take damage
   * Players can switch positions using Reposition
   * Melee attacks can only hit adjacent spaces (orthogonal)
   * Ranged attacks can hit any non-adjacent space
   * Special attacks can hit any spaces
* Each turn, each player draws 6 cards to start
* Each action has a cost attached to them, which is discarding a player’s hand
* Players can go in any order
* When everyone is done, the enemy attacks and deals damage accordingly
   * All enemy attacks hit the front row first, if it’s empty, they will hit the back row
   * Wide attacks hit the unit in front and adjacent to them
   * Row attacks hit all units in that row
   * Enemy has up to 6 different attacks based on dice rolled
   * Each attack has an area that can be hit
   * Also different HP pools depending on number of player
   * Enemies can take up multiple spaces, making them susceptible to more damage
* Heal after every combat? Level up actions?


Elemental Tokens
Wind (buff): discard to Reposition at no cost
Poison (debuff): unit takes 1 damage at the end of its phase, not discarded
Earth (buff): discard as 1 energy when playing cards
Water (buff): discard to heal 1 HP during Hero Phase
Fire (debuff): discard to deal 3 damage during Enemy Phase
Lightning (buff): the unit’s next attack do +1 damage
Light (buff): the unit receives -1 damage from the next attack
Dark (debuff): the unit takes +1 damage from the next attack


Paladin (12 HP)
Talent: Farore’s Blessing
Gain 1 blessing at the start of combat. You use blessing to do one of the following at 1 cost once per turn:
* Renew: Heal 2 HP to any ally
* Smite: 4S
* Aegis: 3 block to any ally


Starting cards:
* Invocation (1 cost) x2
   * Reposition, gain 1 blessing
* Slash (1 cost) x3
   * 2M
* Shield Bash (1 cost) x2
   * 1M and 2 block
* Wide Guard (2 cost) x1
   * Distribute 5 block across any allies
* Meditate (0 cost) x1
   * 1 block, gain 1 blessing
* Rally (1 cost) x1
   * You deal +2 damage and adjacent allies deal +1 damage this turn
* Luminous (0 cost) x2
   * If this card was discarded for a blessing, +1 to the effect


Passives:
* Divine Smite: Your Smites now also inflict 1 Light


Ultimates (4 cost):
* Transcendence: Use one of your blessings 4 times at no cost.


Assassin (10 HP)
Talent: Slip Stream
Every time you Reposition, you gain 1 momentum (3 max).
* When you deal M, you add 2M for each momentum you have
* When you take damage, you gain 1 block for each momentum you have
You lose all momentum after


Starting cards:
* Swiftness (0 cost) x2
   * Reposition, gain 1 block
* Stab (1 cost) x3
   * 2M
* Quick Attack (2 cost) x2
   * 1M, regain all the momentum spent
* Acrobatics (1 cost) x2
   * Reposition, then gain 1 momentum
* Parry (1 cost) x1
   * 2 block and draw 1 card
* Dual Daggers (1 cost) x1
   * 1M and 1R
* Wind Wall (1 cost) x1
   * Lose all momentum and gain 2 block for each momentum lost


Passives:
* Tailwind: At the start of each turn, gain 1 Wind


Ultimates (4 cost):
* Assassinate: Double your current momentum (can go above max), then 5M


Gladiator (12 HP)
Talent: Battle Frenzy
Every time you deal or take damage, you receive 1 rage (7 max).
* At max rage, you enter Berserk and draw 3 cards
* During Berserk, all your M and R attacks do +3 damage
* At the end of Hero Phase, you exit Berserk and lose all rage


Starting cards:
* Tactical Retreat (1 cost) x2
   * Reposition, 1M
* Thrust (1 cost) x3
   * 2M / 1R
* Second Wind (1 cost) x2
   * +2 HP, lose 1 rage
* Rapid Jab (1 cost) x1
   * 1M / 1R x3
* Wild Abandon (1 cost) x1
   * Take 1M damage, then deal 5M
* Defensive Stance (1 cost) x2
   * 3 block in front row, or 2 block and 1R in back row
* Polearm Strike (0 cost) x1
   * 1M or 1 block


Passives:
* Energy Surge: Upon entering Berserk, gain 2 Lightning


Ultimates (4 cost):
* Ragnarok: Enter Berserk, gain 2 Lightning, lose all your Rage and heal 1 for every Rage lost.


Huntress (10 HP)
Talent: Huntress’s Mark
Your companion, Mark, joins you in battle and can occupy any space. Mark can’t take damage or be killed.
* If on an ally, Mark provides 2 block to that ally at the end of Hero Phase
* If on an enemy, it receives +1 damage from all sources
* Mark can move anywhere when the Huntress is swapped


Talent: Swift Quiver
Gain 3 Swift Arrows at the start of combat. You can use up any number of Swift Arrows at any time during Hero Phase for the following:
* 1 Swift Arrow: 1S
* 3 Swift Arrows: Reposition
* 5 Swift Arrows: Draw 2 cards
